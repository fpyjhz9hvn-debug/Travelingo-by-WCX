## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a warm, editorial, and effortless aesthetic designed for modern travel discovery and bespoke expedition curation. The visual tone balances grounded editorial confidence with approachable warmth, evoking curiosity, wanderlust, reassurance, and effortless organization.

The design movement combines **Minimalism** with an **Editorial Warmth** motif:
- Pure monochrome primitives (`#000000` text and actionable elements on clean `#FFFFFF` and warm creamy parchment layers like `#F5F4EC` and `#FBF8F2`).
- Bold, expressive editorial sans-serif headers paired with clean, accessible legibility for body narratives and details.
- High-contrast pill-shaped action triggers with generous hit targets.
- Softly sculpted surface cards accented with understated borders, giving structure without visual clutter.

## Colors

The color palette centers on a disciplined high-contrast monochrome scheme softened by organic, warm cream washes.

- **Primary (`#000000`)**: Governs high-impact typographic hierarchy, primary interaction nodes, dark footer areas, and high-visibility step indicators.
- **Secondary (`#F5F4EC`)**: A signature warm stone/parchment tone used for hero section highlights, thematic callouts, and subtle tonal switches between page sections.
- **Neutral (`#666666`)**: Balanced mid-tone gray for metadata, sub-copy, body captions, and unselected structural items.
- **Cream Subtle (`#FBF8F2`) & Surface Light (`#FAFAFA`)**: Ambient background foundations delivering softness under photography without the sterile harshness of untreated white.
- **Footer Surface (`#0C0C0C`)**: An immersive deep-black canvas terminating the journey experience with clear white contrast.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is built upon a contrast between **Epilogue** and **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next**:

- **Epilogue**: Gives headers, kickers, numbers, and CTAs their deliberate, structured, and editorial presence. Its geometric structure paired with humanist proportions anchors section headers and key numbers.
- **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next**: Serves as the reading engine for paragraphs, destination briefs, and multi-line testimonials, ensuring frictionless legibility across all display densities.
- **Kicker / Eyebrow**: Always styled in uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.08em`) to systematically label the thematic section ahead of the primary headline.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on a fluid-responsive 12-column grid system on desktop (`max-width: 1240px`) with 24px gutters, transforming into a 4-column system on mobile devices (`< 768px`) with 16px gutters and 20px screen margins.

Vertical rhythm adheres strictly to an intentional kicker-to-header rhythm:
- Eyebrow labels sit `space-xs` above headlines.
- Headlines maintain `space-sm` to descriptive body copy.
- Section intervals breathe deeply with `space-xxl` (80px) on desktop and `space-xl` (56px) on mobile, ensuring a paced storytelling cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy drop shadows or skeuomorphic bevels; it is achieved through tonal stratification and whisper-light containment lines:

- **Flat Surface Stratification**: Depth is articulated through shifting surface tones: transitioning seamlessly from `#FFFFFF` to warm `#F5F4EC` or `#FBF8F2`.
- **Understated Outlines**: Cards, step panels, and input fields use a crisp 1px solid hairline (`#E5E2D8` on light canvas, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` on dark canvas).
- **Subtle Ambient Lift (Hover State Only)**: Cards and interactive modules elevate on pointer hover using an ambient low-contrast blur: `0px 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`, keeping the physical metaphor quiet and modern.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a high-radius silhouette philosophy:
- **Interactive Controls (Pill)**: Buttons, search inputs, badges, and social media pills utilize a full pill radius (`border-radius: 9999px`) to create tactile, soft touchpoints.
- **Card Containers**: Destination cards, step blocks, and blog items adopt comfortable, rounded geometry (`1.25rem` to `1.5rem` / 20px–24px) that frames photography without sharp corners.
- **Numbered Badges**: Step markers use squircle or compact rounded square geometry (`0.5rem` / 8px) to contrast against pill buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid `#000000` background with pure white typography, full pill radius (`9999px`), vertical padding of `0.875rem`, horizontal padding of `1.75rem`. Hover state transitions to `#222222` with a slight upward translation (`-1px`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Button**: Transparent background with a 1.5px solid `#000000` outline, black typography, full pill radius.
- **Inverted Dark Button**: Pure white background with black typography for use on dark footers and photo overlays.

### Badges & Eyebrows
- **Category Pill Badges**: Warm cream `#F5F4EC` surface, `#000000` label text, full pill radius, compact padding (`0.375rem 0.875rem`).
- **Rating / Social Proof Strip**: Avatar stack overlapping by `-8px`, paired with 5 solid star indicators and bold counter labels.

### Cards
- **Destination & Blog Cards**: Vertical stack with a clipped image container (`border-radius: 1rem`), internal date/location pill overlay, bold title, and inline `→ Read More` text link.
- **Service Feature Cards**: Centered or left-aligned monochrome line icons within an airy card, followed by an Epilogue subhead and Atkinson body copy.
- **Process Step Cards**: Prominent dark square step index (`1`, `2`, `3`, `4`) with subtle watermark icon art, clean dividing borders, and concise descriptive copy.

### Accordions (FAQ)
- Clean horizontal rows defined by a 1px top border (`#E5E2D8`).
- Header displays bold title with an accordion state trigger (`+` when collapsed, `×` when expanded) aligned to the right.
- Smooth ease-out transition revealing body explanation copy.

### Input Fields & Newsletter Forms
- Pill-shaped or soft-rounded enclosure with dark internal surface or crisp light border (`#E5E2D8`), subtle placeholder text (`#666666`), and an integrated full-width or inline pill submit button.
