Frequently Asked Questions

Get Clarity Before Booking Your Next Adventure

Find answers to common questions regarding bookings, itineraries, and services.

How do I book a trip with TravFrick? ✕

Simply fill out our trip planner or contact our team directly. We'll tailor a travel plan just for you and handle the whole booking process once you're 100% satisfied with the itinerary.

Can I customize my travel itinerary? +

Absolutely! Every trip is fully customized to match your tempo, personal interests, hotel tier preferences, and budget constraints.

Is customer support available during the trip? +

Yes, our dedicated travel concierge support desk is on standby 24 hours a day, 7 days a week via WhatsApp, phone, and email to assist you in real time.

Do you handle flight bookings too? +

Yes! We find the fastest and most convenient airfare routes with verified airlines, coordinating airport transfers directly to your hotel.

Are there any hidden fees? +

No hidden costs. All prices, inclusions, taxes, and service fees are completely transparent in your personalized quotation before any payment is confirmed.
